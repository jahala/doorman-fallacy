# Brief: who the site is for, and what content they need

Status: delegated by Jan on 2026-10-19 ("use /jev-ux-review … also use your own intelligence, since Jev isn't a perfect judge"). Claude wrote the brief and judges the findings; Jan decides.

**Mode:** a built product (the site at commit dee4d98), plus candidate content not yet built.

**Questions this run answers, and the decision each feeds:**
1. Which readers come to which page at all? Feeds: which pages earn the most polish, and which can stay plain.
2. For each reader, which content blocks (built and candidate) would they look for and use, and how much do they matter? Feeds: what to build next, and what to cut or demote.
3. What should a first-time visitor see first on the home page? Feeds: the home page's order.

**Not in scope:** filling the ledger with cases (Jan, 2026-10-19).

**Kill criterion:** if my own spot-check rejects more than half of the findings, the method is reported as not useful here and the decisions rest on judgment alone. Calibration items (a bread recipe, a staff login) must come out near zero, and the case table must come out high for the journalist and the researcher; if not, the descriptions are fixed before reading anything else.
