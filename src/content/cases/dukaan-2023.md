## The role

Dukaan's customer support executives handled queries.[^web3cafe-2023] The team had 26 members.[^yahoo-bi-2023]

## The change

Suumit Shah, Dukaan's chief executive, laid off 90 per cent of his support staff and replaced them with an AI chatbot.[^yahoo-bi-2023-2] Shah told Insider that the layoffs took place in September 2022 and that the company let go of 23 of the 26 members of its customer support team.[^yahoo-bi-2023] Insider said it could not independently verify the figures.[^yahoo-bi-2023-3]

Shah said Lina could answer common questions about Dukaan instantly and accurately.[^ie-2023] The chatbot fell short on account-specific questions, such as why a payout had been pending for two days.[^ie-2023-2] The chief executive said a later demo answered both common and account-specific questions.[^web3cafe-2023-2]

## What happened next

Asked whether the laid-off staff were given any help, he said further details would be revealed in a LinkedIn post.[^web3cafe-2023-3] By 14 July 2023, Shah had defended the move, saying the chatbot was more efficient, and said he regretted discussing the layoffs on Twitter.[^national-2023]
