## The role

Klarna said its AI assistant had been responsible for two-thirds of customer service chats.[^cxtoday] The assistant was to support customers with refunds, returns, payment-related issues, cancellations, disputes and invoice inaccuracies.[^cxtoday-2] Klarna said customers could still choose to interact with live agents.[^cxtoday-3]

## The change

Klarna said the assistant was completing the work of 700 employees.[^cxtoday] Klarna said its query resolution time had dropped from 11 minutes to two minutes.[^cxtoday-4]

In December 2023, Klarna's chief executive said the company was not currently hiring at all, apart from engineers.[^cxtoday-5] Klarna cut its workforce from 5,000 to 3,800 during 2023.[^retail-systems] Klarna said changes to its workforce were largely due to natural attrition.[^retail-systems-2]

## What happened next

On 9 May 2025, Retail Systems reported that Klarna's chief executive told Bloomberg that the company's AI-driven job cuts had gone too far.[^retail-systems-3] Klarna was testing a new group of staff in an Uber-type setup who could log in, work remotely and take up customers' requests.[^retail-systems-3]

The chief executive said the business was planning a recruitment drive so that customers could speak to a real person rather than a chatbot.[^retail-systems-4] He said cost seemed to have been too predominant an evaluation factor and that the result was lower quality.[^retail-systems-5]
