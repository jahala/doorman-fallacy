## The role

For decades a pool of workers at the Assago offices, outside Milan, reported the audience of television and radio programmes and catalogued millions of advertising slots across radio, television, newspapers, magazines and cinemas, every day, around the clock.[^collettiva] The company began monitoring the share of the Sanremo Festival in 1986.[^affaritaliani]

## The change

The company communicated its intention to proceed with the dismissal of 42 people out of a total workforce of 136 belonging to the legal entity Nielsen Media Service Italy.[^collettiva-2] The procedure started on 31 March.[^collettiva-3] According to a note circulated by the Rsu and Filcams, the company communicated its intention to dismiss 43 people on a total workforce of 136 in the legal entity Nielsen Media Service Italy.[^quotidiano]

Company notes named the move to automation and the optimisation of processes as its new pillars.[^affaritaliani-2] The employees said that the workers would no longer produce the audience data of the next edition of the Festival, and that an artificial intelligence algorithm would take their place in this operation.[^ilfatto]

Giuseppe Nardozza, a Filcams Cgil representative and responsible for quality control of the statistical panel, said that for over eighteen months the company had carried out a drastic plan to expand AI, with staff reductions and offshoring across the European area.[^affaritaliani-3]

Workers met in assembly and declared a state of agitation, announcing moments of protest and mobilisation.[^quotidiano-2] Nardozza said the quality level risked a vertical collapse and that the company was depleting its know-how.[^affaritaliani-4]

## What happened next

A first meeting between the parties was scheduled for 13 April at Confcommercio in Milan.[^collettiva-4]
