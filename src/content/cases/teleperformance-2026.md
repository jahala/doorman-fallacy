## The role

Velsia is the new name of Teleperformance, one of the world's leaders in outsourced customer relations, that is, telephone call centres.[^ruffin-jul-2026] A written question in the National Assembly said the job was already difficult, with whole days spent on the telephone.[^ruffin-jul-2026-2]

## The change

Under its Future Forward plan, TP was preparing to cut 3,300 posts this year, mainly in Europe and North Africa.[^linforme]

TP says AI agents take on routine consumer interactions while human specialists focus on complex cases.[^tp-awards-apr-2026] Velsia's staff have to work with artificial intelligence in their daily work.[^ici-jul-2026]

## What happened next

The same written question said 598 of 1,860 posts had already been cut, and only four sites remained in France.[^ruffin-jul-2026-3] In 2025 the group set up a large voluntary departure plan across France, and the Le Mans headcount has fallen since the start of the year.[^ici-jul-2026-2] Velsia's HR director for France, Jean-François Mariotte, sought to reassure staff at the Le Mans site.[^ici-jul-2026-3] According to him, the company is doing well, and 125 people have been hired in France since the start of the year.[^ici-jul-2026-4]

## Open questions

- Did the 3,300 cuts happen as reported, and in which countries?
- Did complaint volumes, repeat calls or call waits change after AI took the routine work?
- Have any sites hired back agents, or reversed the change?
- What happened to the 598 French posts and the four remaining sites?
