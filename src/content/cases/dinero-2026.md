## The role

Martin Thorborg is the chief executive of the accounting company Dinero.[^dr] He said that in customer support they have worked intensively for three years on solving the tasks with AI.[^dr-2] The positions Dinero is not refilling this year are support jobs held by student assistants.[^dr-3]

## The change

Martin Thorborg is not refilling a further 10 positions this year.[^dr-4] The students' positions are not refilled when they finish their studies.[^dr-3] 24Victoria reported that about 30 jobs have gone for good.[^24v]

Over the last six months, the AI has become able to take just over 71 per cent of all customer enquiries, he told P1 Morgen.[^dr-5]

## What happened next

On a scale from 0 to 100, the AI support scores 90 for satisfaction and the human scores one point more, according to their own figures.[^dr-6]
