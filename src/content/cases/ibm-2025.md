## The role

A time analysis found that 65% of the work HR business partners did for front-line managers was transactional, such as answering questions about the maternity leave policy.[^modern-exec] HR business partners spent too much time on routine work for too many employees, which lowered their engagement scores and the net promoter scores of the managers they served.[^modern-exec-2]

## The change

IBM had an early version of a chatbot in place, but after a year, no one was using it.[^modern-exec-3] Shortly after AskHR launched, IBM told all 21,000 first-line managers worldwide that dedicated HR business partner support would no longer be available.[^ibm-case-study] The global HR email address and phone number were shut down.[^ibm-case-study-2] Managers could only access support through AskHR.[^ibm-case-study-3]

Between 2021 and 2024, AskHR became the single entry point for first-line managers and 85% of upline managers.[^ibm-case-study-4] Over a seven-year period, IBM went from 1,500 HR business partners serving 29,000 managers and executives to 350 serving roughly the same size group.[^modern-exec-4] The reduction was largely done by redeploying HRBPs to more complex work, not backfilling voluntary attrition, and long-term performance management.[^modern-exec-5]

## What happened next

In 2025, AskHR handled more than 16 million individual user messages.[^ibm-case-study-5] IBM hired more programmers and salespeople.[^pymnts]

The remaining 6% of cases, described as the ethical calls and the real judgment, still land with people.[^hr-chief]
