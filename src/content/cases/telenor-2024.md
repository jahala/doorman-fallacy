## The role

Telenor had two units in Rørvik: a customer centre with 35 employees, and other work with 37 employees, mainly invoicing and payment for Telenor Norge.[^telenor-pr] Telenor said only the customer centre was covered by the change, and that it would still have a presence in Rørvik afterwards.[^telenor-pr-2]

## The change

On 3 April 2024, Telenor Norge announced changes in three divisions.[^telenor-pr-3] The managing director of Telenor Norge, Birgitte Engebretsen, said the changes would lead to organisational change, the loss of roles and the loss of manual tasks.[^telenor-pr-4] The changes would take effect from 1 May and mean a reduction of just over 100 employees.[^telenor-pr-5] As part of the changes, the customer centre in Rørvik would close.[^telenor-pr-6] About one in twelve employees working in customer service would get help to find other work.[^ta]

Telenor described artificial intelligence as one of several technologies it uses, for example in customer service with its award-winning chatbot Telmi.[^ta-2] Engebretsen said this would also reduce the need for manual chat.[^ta-3] According to Telenor, the new chatbot is more cost-effective than manual customer service.[^ta-4]

In a press release, Telenor said its office in Kristiansand would close from 1 September 2025.[^nrk] The office had 78 employees, 58 of whom worked in customer service.[^nrk-2] Telenor's HR director, Elisabeth Stene, told NRK that the company was there to tell employees it would close the office premises.[^nrk-3] Stene said digitalisation had reduced the need for manual tasks, particularly in customer service.[^nrk-4] The closure would make several customer service employees redundant.[^nrk-5] Customer service employees who wished to would be offered a move to another workplace.[^nrk-6] Kenneth Pettersen was the senior representative for El og IT Forbundet at Telenor Norge.[^nrk-7] He said the union had worked to keep the site at Kjøita.[^nrk-8]

## What happened next

The employees in Rørvik had had their last working day a short time earlier.[^frifagbevegelse]
