## The role

The proofreading and page-layout services are the most affected.[^france3-oct]

Jérôme Morin, elected for the SNJ to the paper's works council, said the plan changes the nature of the work, with the machine doing the rewriting of some articles and the ordering of the information.[^france3-oct-2] He said the secrétaires de rédaction lose half their posts.[^france3-oct-3] The other half will have to proofread with the help of AI tools.[^france3-oct-4]

## The change

Groupe EBRA announced a voluntary-departure plan of up to 400 posts across its nine regional titles.[^france3-jun]

At Le Progrès, 60 posts are to be cut, 49 of them in the newsroom.[^france3-oct-5] 23 posts are to be created, according to the SNJ.[^france3-oct-6]

The strikers also opposed the arrival of AI software to lay out the paper automatically and so replace part of the secrétaires de rédaction.[^ici]

## What happened next

The employees of Le Progrès went on strike on Friday 9 October.[^france3-oct-7] 82% of the journalists, 113 people, were on strike, according to the union.[^france3-oct-8]

Morin said correspondents will have little choice but to press the generative AI's proofreading button, because they are given seven minutes per correspondent article.[^france3-oct-9]

Negotiations between the unions and management were under way, until 2 November 2026.[^ici-2] The reorganisation is announced for early 2027.[^ici-3]
