## The role

The anti-money-laundering checks division has about 6,000 staff.[^dutchnews] The checks largely involve reviewing customer transactions according to fixed procedures.[^dutchnews-2] Banks are legally required to carry out these checks.[^dutchnews-2]

## The change

ING plans to cut about 1,250 jobs worldwide this year.[^dutchnews-3] The presentation to investors suggested that part of the reduction will be in anti-money-laundering checks.[^dutchnews] ING said new technology, including AI, should make it possible to automate more of this work.[^dutchnews-4]

ING had earlier filed a forecast with the UWV for cutting up to 950 positions in its Dutch division.[^banken] The cuts are part of a global savings programme aimed at reducing costs by €350 million by 2026.[^dutchnews-5]

## What happened next

On 20 March 2026, Duygu Akcay of FNV Finance said efficiency gains were not being turned into shorter working weeks, such as the 32-hour week.[^banken-2]
