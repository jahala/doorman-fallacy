## The role

The jobs cut were mainly in call-centre areas.[^xataka-may] A large part of the dismissals affected workers affiliated to the Sitatyr union.[^xataka-may-2]

Xataka México reported that the call-centre area had for years carried operational problems and constant criticism from users about the quality of service.[^xataka-may-3]

## The change

The company would replace its traditional call centres with automated systems based on artificial intelligence.[^xataka-mar] The company accelerated the replacement of human operators with AI systems.[^xataka-may]

According to Francisco Valim, chief executive of Izzi and Sky, artificial intelligence had already formed part of customer service for some months.[^xataka-mar-2] The executive said that, because of significant results, the company decided to commit to full integration of AI.[^xataka-mar-3]

The technology came from companies including Oracle, Salesforce and Amazon Web Services.[^xataka-may-4] Valim did not say how many jobs would be lost.[^xataka-mar-4]

## What happened next

At the close of 2025 the company eliminated 980 positions, equal to 15.5% of the workforce it reported at the end of 2023.[^eltiempo-may]
