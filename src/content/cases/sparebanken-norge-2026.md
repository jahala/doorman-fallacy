## The role

Through the credit tool Kred, 60 per cent of all retail loan cases are now fully digital.[^krs-ki] In practice, only the deviations in today's loan process are handled by people.[^krs-ki-2]

## The change

Sparebanken Norge has started a transformation programme.[^e24] As part of it, the bank plans to reduce staffing in some divisions by between 100 and 120 full-time equivalents.[^e24-2] The measures will be carried out during 2026.[^e24-3] The full-time equivalents are to be cut in staff and support functions, personal banking and direct banking.[^e24-4] Overall, the number of full-time equivalents in the bank will probably stay at about today's level over time.[^e24-5]

The chief executive, Jan Erik Kjerpeseth, told DN that it is about profit being under pressure.[^kode24] He also pointed to new technology such as artificial intelligence, which he said would replace several work tasks.[^kode24-2] It could be anti-money-laundering work, spot checks or development of case-handling tools in customer advice.[^kode24-3]

## What happened next

Pål Behrens is head of working life at Finansforbundet, the country's largest trade union in finance.[^ba] He said they believe there is no basis for dismissals, and that the bank must solve it through restructuring or voluntary severance packages.[^ba-2] The employees deny that artificial intelligence has already created a need to cut staff.[^kode24-4]

## Open questions

- How many full-time equivalents had gone by the end of 2026, and which divisions carried the reduction?
- What did the bank report at its capital markets day in August 2026 about the programme's financial effects?
- Did direct banking or personal banking report longer waits, more complaints or more errors after the roles went?
- Did the anti-money-laundering work and spot checks that moved to artificial intelligence produce cases that people had to take up?
- Did the customer divisions grow as the bank said, so that total headcount stayed level?
