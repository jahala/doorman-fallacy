## The role

DNB said it has adopted agentic AI in several parts of its operations.[^dnb-release] DNB said this technology replaces tasks that were previously handled manually.[^dnb-release-2] DNB named control of customer data, Know Your Customer work, and technology development and coding as areas where AI agents can contribute substantial efficiency gains.[^dnb-release-3]

## The change

DNB said the changes entail a reduction in the workforce of about 400 full-time equivalents.[^dnb-release-4] DNB said it will adapt the organisation of Technology & Services and its skills mix.[^dnb-release-5]

DNB said it will carry out the process in close dialogue with the employee representatives.[^dnb-release-6]

## What happened next

On 6 October 2026, DNB said the downsizing will be completed during the fourth quarter of 2026.[^dnb-release-7] DNB said further information about the financial effects will be disclosed later this year.[^dnb-release-8]
