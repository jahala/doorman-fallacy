## The role

Mercado Libre dismissed 119 employees in Latin America, 32 of them in Argentina.[^infogremiales] The cuts fell mainly on UX writers, the role that designs content and user journeys inside the platforms.[^infogremiales-2] According to those accounts, almost the whole content team was dismissed, the designers were kept, and the designers were asked to take on broader tasks including writing.[^eleconomista]

Workers from different countries were called to mass meetings on Zoom, where a Human Resources statement was read in Spanish and Portuguese.[^gizmodo] After the call they lost immediate access to their accounts and work tools.[^gizmodo-2] Lola Rodríguez, one of the dismissed workers, said the company told them it was seeking generalist roles.[^tiempoar] The message also said the company would no longer consider people who only write, design or draw screens, and would ask everyone for everything.[^tiempoar-2]

During 2025, UX workers were assigned to train the artificial intelligence model as part of their job.[^tiempoar-3] Former employees said they spent much of their work training automated systems that now answer customers and sellers, including through WhatsApp.[^eleconomista-2] Professionals who stayed on the team would have access to new AI tools, and designers were expected to take on part of the tasks previously done by UX writers.[^fastcompanybrasil]

## The change

Mercado Libre said it was evolving the organisation of its UX teams to integrate the Design and Content areas more effectively.[^infogremiales-3] It said the transformation aimed at more agile and collaborative structures, using technology to keep improving the user experience.[^infogremiales-4] The company said the measure did not change its growth plans in the country.[^fastcompanybrasil-2] It said the departures were less than 0.09% of its more than 125,000 employees.[^bloomberglinea]

Many of those dismissed said the process was directly linked to artificial intelligence.[^eleconomista-3] People with knowledge of the matter, who asked not to be named, said the company had explicitly pushed the use of AI tools to raise productivity, especially in Content and Design.[^bloomberglinea-2] Those sources said teams had trained models and bots such as Gemini and GPT for writing, ideation, experience flows and style guides.[^bloomberglinea-3] The Association of Computer Workers in Argentina (AGC) linked the dismissals to the use of artificial intelligence, citing the testimony of employees dismissed in the country.[^bloomberglinea-4] In a note published on its website, the union said testimony from dismissed workers and an analysis of the tasks affected suggested AI tools had been behind the decision.[^bloomberglinea-5]

## What happened next

By 16 January 2026, users and sellers were reporting more interactions handled by automated agents, especially through channels such as WhatsApp.[^gizmodo-3] Some said simple problems were resolved only after insisting until they reached a human operator.[^gizmodo-4]

The SINDPD/SC union said the company refused collective negotiation after the mass dismissals.[^sindpdsc] It said the company's labour relations representative, Vanessa Soares, told it Mercado Libre would not hold collective talks.[^sindpdsc-2] According to the company, any talks would be handled only individually, through Railton Costa Carvalho, a Legal Corp senior manager at the company.[^sindpdsc-3]

A lawyer represented a former Mercado Libre employee in a case seeking reinstatement to the company's staff.[^reporterbrasil] The case was rejected at the 2nd Labour Court of Osasco and accepted on appeal.[^reporterbrasil-2] The judge understood that the episode corresponded to a mass dismissal, without notification to the legal unions, which would invalidate the dismissal.[^reporterbrasil-3] The judge ordered Mercado Libre to re-admit the worker.[^reporterbrasil-4] The company can appeal.[^reporterbrasil-5]
