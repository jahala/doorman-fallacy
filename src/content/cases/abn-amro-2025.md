## The role

The planned reorganisation mainly affected support staff across the whole bank.[^cfo] Employment in customer service, the operations department and the team that checks for money laundering was to be reduced by about 35 per cent.[^cfo-2] Less human capacity was needed, mainly for repetitive or administrative tasks.[^customerfirst]

## The change

ABN Amro would reduce its workforce by 5,200 net full-time positions by 2028, compared with the number of people employed at the end of the previous year.[^cfo-3] Around half the job losses would come through not replacing staff who leave the company.[^dutchnews-cuts] The bank's chief executive, Marguerite Bérard, said the bank was fully committed to supporting everyone affected with a social plan.[^dutchnews-cuts-2] Bérard said most of the jobs to go involved support staff and that AI could take over some of their work.[^dutchnews-unions]

## What happened next

Full-time equivalents fell by 528 in the first quarter of 2026.[^wealthbriefing-q1] The total FTE decrease since the end of 2024 stood at about 40 per cent of the bank's target for 2028.[^wealthbriefing-q1-2] A summarising tool helps client advisers reduce the time spent after each call by up to 50 per cent.[^wealthbriefing-q1-3]

In the second quarter the bank launched its first GenAI-powered client voice bot, to answer client questions and give guidance on selected topics.[^ariva-q2] It also introduced a GenAI knowledge assistant for Know Your Customer and anti-money-laundering analysts.[^ariva-q2-2]
