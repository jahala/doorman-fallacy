## The role

AI agents took over thousands of tasks once handled by humans in Salesforce customer support.[^channelnews] Agentforce is the platform that deploys agents to handle queries, follow up on leads and escalate complex cases to human employees.[^channelnews-2]

## The change

Benioff said he reduced support from 9,000 heads to about 5,000 because he needed fewer heads.[^channelnews-3] He said AI was now handling up to half of customer conversations.[^channelnews-4]

Salesforce said it had redeployed hundreds of employees into other functions such as sales, professional services and customer success.[^channelnews-5] Salesforce still employs about 76,000 staff globally.[^channelnews-6]

## What happened next

Salesforce said it did not lay off 4,000 employees.[^itbrief] Salesforce said the restructuring included internal redeployment of employees, decisions not to backfill certain roles and natural attrition.[^itbrief-2]

Critics said Agentforce proved unreliable in customer deployments.[^itbrief-3] Salesforce said it has closed more than 18,500 Agentforce deals, with over 9,500 already in paid production.[^itbrief-4]
