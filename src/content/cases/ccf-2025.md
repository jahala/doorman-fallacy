## The role

The job protection plan provided for 1,250 departures and did not concern any commercial adviser.[^lefigaro] The plan was to cut 500 jobs at the Paris head office and in support functions, and more than 700 in the bank branches.[^csematin]

## The change

CCF launched the plan in 2024 to return to balance from 2026.[^lefigaro] CCF's chief executive, Niccolò Ubertalli, said the use of AI had removed jobs at the bank.[^lefigaro-2] He said the jobs were in the part of the bank that is not client-facing.[^lefigaro-3]

A CCF spokesperson contacted by AFP said AI had no link with the job protection plan.[^lefigaro-4] The restructuring included the progressive integration of AI solutions into the automation of operational tasks.[^boursorama-2026-07]

## What happened next

The first wave led to 700 departures, 93% of them voluntary, CCF said.[^lefigaro-5] CCF reported a net loss of 575 million euros for 2025, weighed down by its transformation plan.[^boursorama-2026-02] A further wave of departures was expected in 2026 as part of the 1,250 planned.[^boursorama-2026-02-2]

CCF returned to profit in the first half of 2026 with 62 million euros.[^boursorama-2026-07-2] Its chief executive said the bank was profitable a year ahead of the plan.[^boursorama-2026-07-3]
