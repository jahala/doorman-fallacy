## The role

The 45 roles were in CBA's Customer Service Direct business.[^acs-reversal] Cyber Daily reported that CBA had announced a new customer assistance AI voice bot system only a month before the redundancies.[^cyberdaily-cuts]

## The change

CBA said it would make at least 45 roles redundant after its push to use AI.[^cyberdaily-cuts]

Cyber Daily reported that it was the first time the Finance Sector Union had been notified of redundancies related to AI.[^cyberdaily-cuts-2]

## What happened next

On 21 August 2025, CBA confirmed that its decision that the 45 customer service jobs were no longer needed was incorrect.[^acs-reversal-2] A CBA spokesperson said the initial assessment did not adequately consider all relevant business considerations and that this error meant the roles were not redundant.[^acs-reversal] CBA said it had apologised to the employees concerned.[^acs-reversal-3]

The Finance Sector Union said the affected workers would be given the choice to continue in their current role, be redeployed within the bank, or continue with their redundancy.[^acs-reversal-4] The union also said calls to the bank's customer service workers were increasing, and management were offering overtime and getting team leaders to answer some calls.[^acs-reversal-5]

CBA said it was also reviewing its internal processes.[^acs-reversal-6]

## Open questions
